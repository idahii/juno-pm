# Human Evaluation Rubric · Juno

_Module 5. Generated by `M5 - Human Evaluation Rubric.html`. Paste over `05-evals/human-rubric.md`._

## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Juno's weekly feedback digest
- **Value proposition:** Turn the week's customer feedback into the main problems I need to know about, each with a quote I can check.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy | Does every quote in the digest appear word-for-word in the channel this week? | Yes / No | At least one quote is paraphrased, invented, or from outside the 7-day window | n/a | Every quote matches a real message exactly |
| Coverage | Did the digest catch every problem that had 3 or more messages behind it? | Yes / No | The scan finds at least one problem with 3+ messages that is not in the digest or the watch list | n/a | A 5-minute scan of the channel finds nothing with 3+ messages that the digest missed |
| Prioritization | Is the top problem the one with the most evidence or the most business impact? | 1 to 5 | The top problem is not the one with the most messages or impact, and I can't see why it's there | Top 2 are right, the rest I'd reorder.  | This is the order I'd tell leadership. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** digests a week, including any that Juno flagged as medium or low confidence.
- **Who scores them with you:** Me, plus one other PM every week.
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** If our scores differ by 2 or more on a scale question, or we disagree on a yes/no, a third PM breaks the tie and we rewrite that description so it's clearer next time.

## Pass bar

Every yes/no question passes, and an average of 4 or higher on grouping, prioritization, and usefulness across the week's sample.

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- A customer name, email, or company name in the digest. A digest posted outside the channel it was called from. A Jira ticket created without PM confirmation.
