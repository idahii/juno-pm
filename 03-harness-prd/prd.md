## Problem & user

RocketShip PMs need this handled: Juno is RocketShip's AI Associate PM. Juno reads a week of feedback from Slack and Jira and posts a short digest of the main problems when the PM types /digest.. Juno does this inside limits a human set on purpose, and defends every call it makes.

## 01 Context · Data Requirements

**Required sources, and exclusions**

Sources:
-Slack: #product-feedback and #support, last 7 days, pulled when /digest runs
-Jira: issues labelled customer-feedback, created or updated in the last 7 days
The RocketShip Product Strategy One-Pager, so Juno knows which problems matter this quarter

Excluded: DMs, #sales-private, Notion, and anything older than 7 days. Customer names, emails, and company names are stripped before Juno sees the text. A short window keeps the digest about this week, and stripping names means the digest can be forwarded without a privacy check.

**How fresh must it be**

Fetch on demand. Whatever is in the two channels and Jira at the moment /digest is typed is what Juno reads. No index, no cache. If Slack or Jira cannot be reached, Juno does not run on a partial set. It replies "Can't reach Jira right now, try again in a few minutes" and stops, because a digest that quietly skipped a source would look complete and be wrong.

## 02 Tools · System Capabilities

**The verb list**

read_slack_channel(channel, days) — READ
search_jira(label, days) — READ
read_strategy_doc() — READ
draft_digest(signals) — DRAFT
post_to_channel(channel, text) — WRITE, only the channel /digest was typed in
create_jira_ticket(problem, quotes) — WRITE, confirm first

**Deliberate omissions**

No message_user(). Juno never DMs anyone. A digest in a public channel can be corrected by the next person who reads it; a DM cannot.
No update_jira_ticket(). Changing priority or status on a ticket engineers are already working from is how a wrong guess becomes a wrong sprint.
No read_notion(). Tempting for context, but it widens what Juno sees from "this week's feedback" to "everything", and the signal-to-noise collapses.

## 03 Loop · AI Costs & Latency

**Turn ceiling and escalation**

Ceiling: 3 turns per /digest. Turn 1 fetch, turn 2 cluster and draft, turn 3 run the quote check. On turn 3 it must post or stop, never a fourth attempt.
Escalate to the PM when: fewer than 5 messages came back (too thin to group), more than 300 came back (too wide for one digest, ask for a shorter window), or two problems share more than half their quotes (Juno can't tell if that's one problem or two, and that's a judgment call).

**Latency and cost target**

Under 30 seconds from /digest to posted reply, almost every time. The PM typed it and is waiting.
Cost ceiling $0.40 per digest. At roughly 2 runs a week per PM and 10 PMs, that is about $32 a month. Small enough that nobody needs to approve it, which is the point.

## 04 Memory · Data Requirements

**What persists, at what scope**

Per channel, never per person. Three things survive between runs: the problems named in last week's digest (so Juno can say "still open since last week"), the problems the PM marked "known, stop surfacing", and a run log of when it ran and what it read. Nothing about any individual customer or employee is stored.

**Expiry and write rules**

Last week's problems expire after 30 days. "Known" marks live until the PM removes them. Only the PM can write a "known" mark; Juno can only write the run log. If the PM marks something known and Juno's next run still finds it, the PM's mark wins and the problem goes into "weak signals" with a note, not back into the main table.

## 05 Permissions · AI Risks & Mitigations

**The permission tier**

| Side-effect class | Tier |
|---|---|
| read | auto |
| draft | auto |
| write | confirm |
| send | blocked |

**Justification**

Reads are auto because a wrong read costs tokens and nothing else.
Drafts are auto because a draft nobody has posted has harmed nobody.
Posting the digest to the calling channel is the one write that runs without confirm: it's one channel, it's the channel the PM is already in, and the message can be deleted in two seconds. Creating a Jira ticket needs confirm because an engineer might pick it up before the PM reads it.
Sends are blocked. Juno never DMs, emails, or posts to any other channel. A digest that leaves the team can't be taken back, and that combination is never auto.

## 06 Verification · AI Testing & Measurement

**The check before output ships**

Every problem in the digest table must include one quote that appears word-for-word in the messages fetched this run. Paraphrases fail. Quotes from outside the 7-day window fail. The check is a string match, not a model judgment, so it can't be talked around.

**Failure behaviour**

If one quote fails, Juno removes that problem and retries the check once. If it still fails, or if removing it leaves fewer than two problems, Juno posts: "Digest held back. One or more quotes didn't match the channel. Here's what I read, want me to try a shorter window?" with a count of messages fetched. It never rewrites a quote to make it pass, and it never posts a digest with a problem silently removed.
